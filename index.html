Privacy Policy

Block Defense ·17.08.2026

This policy explains what information the Block Defense mobile game ("the Game") collects, why it is collected, and who it is shared with. By installing and playing the Game you agree to the practices described here.

Who we are

The Game is developed and published by Cédric Ragamuffin ("we", "us"), an independent developer. Cédric Ragamuffin is the name shown as the developer of the Game on the app stores, and is the party responsible for the data described in this policy.

For any question about this policy or your data, contact us at info@litstudio.games .

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, address, phone number or email unless you contact us directly. The Game collects the following automatically:

Data

Purpose

Device advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID / Apple IDFA)

Serving ads, measuring ad performance, and limiting how often an ad is shown

Device and app information (model, operating system version, language, region, app version)

Diagnosing crashes and making the Game work correctly across devices

Gameplay events (levels started and completed, progress, in-game currency changes, features used)

Understanding how the Game is played so we can balance and improve it

Crash and performance data

Finding and fixing bugs

Approximate location derived from IP address (country or region level)

Regional ad delivery and analytics; we do not collect precise GPS location

Purchase records (which item was bought, when)

Delivering what you paid for and supporting refund requests

Your game progress, currency balance and settings are stored on your own device. We do not upload or store a copy of your save data.

Payment information

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your card number or billing details.

The Game uses the following services, each of which collects data under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config) — firebase.google.com/support/privacy

  • Unity Ads (advertising network) — unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

  • AppLovin (advertising network) — applovin.com/privacy

Advertising

The Game shows rewarded video ads, which you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward. Our advertising partners may use your advertising identifier to show ads that are more relevant to you and to measure whether an ad worked.

You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking

Turning off personalised advertising does not remove ads from the Game; it means the ads you see are less relevant.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

How long we keep data

Analytics and crash data are retained for as long as they are useful for improving the Game, and in line with the retention settings of the services listed above. Uninstalling the Game removes the data stored on your device.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to how it is processed, or to withdraw consent. To make a request, email info@litstudio.games. We will respond within the period required by the applicable law.

Residents of the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom may also lodge a complaint with their national data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game changes. The date at the top of this page always shows when it was last revised. Material changes will be announced in the Game or on this page.
