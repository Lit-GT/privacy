Privacy Policy – Block Defense

Last updated: 25.10.2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Block Defense (the "Game") handles information when you play it on Android or iOS.

The Game is developed and published by Cédric Ragamuffin, an independent developer ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the data described in this policy. Contact: info@litstudio.games

1. No accounts, no personal details

The Game does not require an account. We do not ask for your name, postal address, phone number or email address. If you contact us directly (for example by email), we receive the information you choose to send us, such as your email address and message, and use it only to answer you.

2. Information collected automatically

When you play, the Game and the third-party services listed in Section 5 automatically collect the following information:

Data Purpose
Advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS where available) Showing ads, frequency capping, ad measurement, and measuring and optimizing our advertising campaigns (attribution)
Device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language, and similar technical data) Diagnosing crashes, ensuring compatibility, ad delivery, and attribution
Gameplay events (for example level completions and tutorial completion) Game balancing and improvements, and, via AppsFlyer, measuring and optimizing our advertising campaigns
Crash and performance data Finding and fixing bugs
Approximate location (country or region, derived from your IP address; we do not collect precise GPS location) Ad delivery, fraud prevention, regional analytics, and attribution
Purchase records (which item was bought, price and currency, and the store transaction reference) Delivering purchases, handling refunds, and, via AppsFlyer, measuring and optimizing our advertising campaigns
Ad revenue data (revenue generated by ads you watch in the Game) Understanding the Game's performance and measuring the return on our advertising campaigns
Install referrer / attribution data (information about which ad or source led to your install) Measuring which advertising campaigns bring players to the Game

3. Save data

Your progress, in-game currency and settings are stored locally on your device. No copy is uploaded to us. Your device's own backup service (for example, Google backup on Android) may include this data in your personal device backup, which is controlled by you and your device provider, not by us.

4. Payments

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your card or billing details.

5. Third-party services

The Game uses the following third-party services. Each of them collects and processes data under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (analytics and crash reporting) – https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Unity LevelPlay / ironSource (ad mediation) and Unity Ads (advertising) – https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Google AdMob (advertising) – https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • AppLovin (advertising network, used to show ads in the Game and to promote the Game in other apps) – https://www.applovin.com/privacy/
  • AppsFlyer (mobile measurement / attribution) – https://www.appsflyer.com/legal/privacy-policy/
  • Google Play Services (Android platform services) – https://policies.google.com/privacy

AppsFlyer and advertising measurement

We use AppsFlyer to measure the performance of our advertising campaigns, for example to understand which ad led to an install. AppsFlyer receives your advertising identifier, device and app information, IP address (used to derive country or region), install referrer data, gameplay events, purchase events and ad revenue data.

AppsFlyer may share install, gameplay event and purchase event data with the advertising networks we use to promote the Game (currently AppLovin), so that they can measure and optimize our campaigns.

6. Advertising

The Game shows rewarded video ads that you choose to watch in exchange for in-game rewards. Ad partners use your advertising identifier to show relevant ads, limit how often you see the same ad, and measure ad performance.

You can control your advertising identifier at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads (or search "Ads" in Settings). You can reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.

Turning off personalized ads does not remove ads from the Game; the ads you see will simply be less relevant to you.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us at info@litstudio.games and we will delete it.

8. Data retention

Analytics, crash and attribution data are kept only as long as they are useful for the purposes described above, subject to the retention settings of the third-party services listed in Section 5. Uninstalling the Game removes the data stored on your device.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to its processing, and to withdraw your consent. To make a request, contact us at info@litstudio.games.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page, and the "Last updated" date at the top will be revised.
